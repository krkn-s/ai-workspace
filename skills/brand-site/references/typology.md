# Typology and core concepts

What a brand site is, where it sits among site types, and how design / UI / UX / brand design relate. Sources at the end.

## Definition

A brand site (*site de marque*) is a site whose mission is neither direct selling (e-commerce) nor single-action conversion (landing page), but making the brand's identity exist: universe, values, personality, territory of expression. It is where the brand fully controls its own narrative — its own art direction, its own editorial tone, brand content (story, commitments, product universe) rather than product sheets or sales arguments.

The brand site has become "the cornerstone of identity, reputation, and trust" (Forbes, 2025); the 2025-2026 trend is the shift from brand sites to brand content systems reclaiming editorial authority from platforms (Pulp Strategy, 2026).

## Site typology

No universally accepted typology exists (documented finding), but this operational classification is consensus:

| Type | Main goal | Success metric | Brand identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand site | Make identity, universe, values exist; awareness and preference | Recall, time on site, branded search | The DA is the product |
| Showcase / institutional | Present an activity, legitimize, contacts | Contact requests, perceived quality | Coherent but sober |
| E-commerce | Sell online | Revenue, conversion, basket size | Subordinate to conversion |
| Landing page | Convert on ONE action (campaign) | Conversion, CPL | Reduced to the message |
| Product / SaaS marketing site | Sell software (demo, trial) | Signups, trials, qualified leads | In service of value proof |
| Web app / product UI | Make people use the service | Retention, activation, tasks | Light: productivity first |
| Media / content site | Audience, ads, SEO | Traffic, engagement, subscriptions | Editorial |

What sets brand sites apart:

- Purpose — building brand capital (image, preference, trust); conversion exists but as an outcome, not the goal.
- Creative bar — the only site type where art direction is the product: "immediately memorable through a visual and tonal identity that is entirely its own, not borrowed from a template" (N4 Studio).
- Formal freedom — editorial layouts, motion design, immersive experiences, as long as readability holds.
- Strategic role — anchor of campaigns, multichannel coherence reference, branded SEO asset.

Boundary: categories blur in practice (a D2C brand site can embed a shop; a SaaS often runs a marketing site separate from the app). The useful question is not "which type is my site?" but "what is its dominant mission, and does the design serve it?"

## Design, UI, UX, brand design

- Design — umbrella term: intentionally conceiving an artifact for a function. "Well designed" means nothing without naming the level.
- UX (user experience) — NN/g: UX encompasses all aspects of the end-user's interaction with the company, its services, and its products. Strategic and structural layer: user research, personas, journeys, wireframes, testing. Site questions: do visitors understand in 5 seconds what the brand is and offers? Can they find their way? Is the path to action frictionless? Does the experience leave a positive, memorable feeling?
- UI (user interface) — the visible, tangible layer: buttons, menus, typography, colors, images, spacing, micro-interactions, states (hover, focus, error). NN/g: the UI is what the product looks like and enables people to interact with it. It materializes UX decisions.
- Brand design — the third layer: translating brand strategy into an expression system — visual identity (logo, palette, type, imagery, grid), verbal identity (name, tone, copywriting), motion identity (animations, transitions, dynamic signature). The layer that separates a brand site from a clean-but-generic site.

Articulation: UX decides what happens and in which order; brand design decides how the brand says it distinctively; UI builds the clickable thing. Failing one undermines the others.

| | UX | UI | Brand design |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | Fluid and understandable? | Beautiful, clear, coherent? | Instantly recognizable as us? |
| Deliverables | Personas, journeys, wireframes, tests | Mockups, components, design system | Brand platform, guidelines, tokens, DA |
| Metrics | Task success, friction, satisfaction | Legibility, consistency, contrast | Recall, preference, attribution |
